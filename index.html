Brain Lab

Think first, then talkPeek inside a robot brain

People don't say every thought out loud before answering, and agents shouldn't either. My "thinker-responder" pattern: a fast, cheap System-1 model makes the silent decision (which sub-agent, which tools), then a System-2 LLM writes the reply.

Your brain has a speedy part that decides what to do, and a careful part that does the talking. My robot helpers work the same way. Pick a question and watch!
